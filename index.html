Our Data Source and Important Information

DMEvia uses publicly available information from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) Medical Equipment Suppliers dataset.

Supplier listings reflect information contained in the public dataset and may not represent current business operations, equipment inventory, delivery coverage, insurance acceptance or eligibility.

Please contact each supplier directly to confirm their current services, equipment availability, location and applicable payment or insurance requirements.

DMEvia is an independent directory. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by CMS or Medicare. DMEvia does not provide medical advice, determine insurance coverage or guarantee supplier services.
