Privacy Policy

How DMEvia collects, uses and handles information.

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DMEvia (https://dmevia.com).

DMEvia is an independent online directory that helps people discover Durable Medical Equipment (DME) supplier locations across the United States.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate our website, respond to enquiries and review supplier submissions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it and when it may be shared with third-party service providers.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information collected through the Contact Form

When you contact DMEvia, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Enquiry category
  • Message submitted through the form

B. Information collected through the Supplier Onboarding Form

Businesses interested in being listed on DMEvia may voluntarily provide:

  • Business name and legal business name
  • Contact person's name
  • Business email address and telephone number
  • Business address and location
  • Website address
  • Business description
  • Equipment categories offered
  • Service areas and delivery information
  • Insurance plans accepted
  • Medicare participation information
  • Other business details requested through the form

Submitting supplier information does not guarantee publication or inclusion in the directory.

C. Information collected automatically

When you visit DMEvia, certain technical information may be processed by our website hosting provider or other infrastructure providers. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Date and time of access
  • Requested website pages or files
  • Technical and security-related information

Our website hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process visitor information as part of its hosting and security operations.

D. ZIP Code Searches

Visitors can search the supplier directory using a five-digit US ZIP code and an equipment category.

Based on the website's current functionality, these searches are processed within the visitor's browser using supplier data files hosted on DMEvia. The search form does not submit the ZIP code to DMEvia's contact or supplier onboarding forms.

3. How We Use Information

We may use submitted information to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries.
  • Review supplier registration requests.
  • Verify and assess business information.
  • Communicate with suppliers about directory listings.
  • Publish approved supplier business information.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated directory information.
  • Maintain, secure and improve the website.
  • Prevent spam, misuse or fraudulent submissions.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not use contact or supplier form submissions to provide medical advice or make medical decisions.

4. Supplier Listings and Public Information

DMEvia uses publicly available supplier information, including information from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

Businesses may also voluntarily submit information through our supplier onboarding form.

Information approved for publication may include business names, addresses, telephone numbers, websites, equipment categories and other relevant business details.

DMEvia may review, update, correct or remove listings where appropriate.

Supplier listings do not guarantee current business operations, equipment availability, delivery coverage, insurance acceptance or Medicare eligibility.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

DMEvia uses third-party services to operate its website and receive form submissions.

GitHub Pages: Website hosting and delivery.

GoDaddy: Domain registration and related domain services.

FormSubmit.co: Processing and forwarding contact and supplier onboarding form submissions to DMEvia's business email address.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies, terms and operational practices.

DMEvia does not control the independent privacy practices, data retention periods or security procedures of these providers.

We encourage visitors to review the relevant providers' privacy information.

6. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

DMEvia's currently reviewed website code does not contain Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or advertising tracking scripts.

We do not currently operate an advertising-based tracking system through the website code reviewed for this policy.

However, hosting providers and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for essential operations, security or service functionality.

The use of Google Search Console for website performance monitoring, if enabled, is separate from Google Analytics.

7. Information Sharing

DMEvia does not intentionally make contact form submissions or private supplier onboarding submissions publicly available.

Information may be shared:

  • With service providers that help operate the website or process submissions.
  • When a supplier has consented to the publication of relevant business information.
  • When disclosure is required by law or a valid legal request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or DMEvia from fraud, abuse or security threats.

We do not sell personal information submitted through our forms.

8. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted to DMEvia.

However, no internet transmission, website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may remain with third-party providers according to their respective retention and deletion practices.

DMEvia may retain submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage supplier listings, maintain business records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations.

9. Sensitive and Medical Information

DMEvia is an equipment supplier directory, not a healthcare provider or medical records service.

Please do not submit:
  • Patient names or medical records
  • Diagnoses or medical histories
  • Prescriptions
  • Insurance identification numbers
  • Social Security numbers
  • Financial account information
  • Other sensitive personal or health information

If you need medical equipment, please communicate sensitive medical information directly with the appropriate healthcare provider or supplier through a suitable secure channel.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact DMEvia to:

  • Ask what personal information you have submitted.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information you previously submitted, where reasonably possible and subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for future publication of supplier information.
  • Ask questions about this Privacy Policy.

Requests can be sent to:

Email: info@dmevia.com

We may need to verify your identity or authority before responding to certain requests.

11. Third-Party Websites

DMEvia may contain links to external websites, including supplier websites and official public data sources.

We are not responsible for the privacy policies, security practices or content of external websites.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

DMEvia is not designed to collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through our forms.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information to DMEvia, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

13. International Visitors

DMEvia is operated from India and provides information about supplier locations in the United States.

Information submitted through our website may be processed or stored by service providers in countries other than the country from which you access the website.

Privacy and data protection laws may differ between countries.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technology or legal obligations change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

15. Contact Us
