About DMEvia

Making it easier to discover Durable Medical Equipment suppliers through accessible public information.

Who We Are

DMEvia is an independent online directory designed to help individuals, families and caregivers find Durable Medical Equipment (DME) supplier locations across the United States.

Finding the right medical equipment supplier can sometimes be challenging. People may need to search through multiple websites and directories to identify suppliers in their area.

DMEvia aims to make that initial search simpler by bringing supplier information and equipment categories together in one easy-to-use website.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make supplier information easier to find and navigate, helping people take a more informed first step when looking for Durable Medical Equipment.

Our guiding principle: Make public supplier information easier to discover, understand and use.

Who Can Use DMEvia?

DMEvia is designed for anyone looking to identify DME suppliers and explore the equipment categories associated with their listed supplier locations.

Patients and Individuals

Explore supplier locations that list equipment categories relevant to your search.

Families and Caregivers

Make it easier to research potential suppliers when helping a family member or someone in your care.

Healthcare Community

Use the directory as an additional starting point when researching DME supplier locations.

Where Does Our Information Come From?

DMEvia uses publicly available supplier information published by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

This information includes supplier details, business addresses and equipment categories reported in the public dataset.

We organize this information to make searching by ZIP code and equipment category more convenient.

Important: Supplier information displayed on DMEvia is based on public data. It may not reflect recent changes, current inventory, delivery coverage, insurance acceptance or actual availability. Users should contact suppliers directly to confirm details.

Our Commitment to Transparency

DMEvia is an independent directory. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by CMS, Medicare or any government agency.

DMEvia does not sell medical equipment, provide medical advice, determine insurance eligibility or guarantee that a listed supplier offers a particular product or service.

Our objective is to provide a convenient starting point for supplier research. Visitors should independently verify supplier information before making decisions.
