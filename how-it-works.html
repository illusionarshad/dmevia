How DMEvia Works

Finding Durable Medical Equipment suppliers can be easier. Follow a few simple steps to explore supplier locations in your area.

Find Suppliers in Four Simple Steps

DMEvia helps you explore supplier locations using publicly available information. You can search by ZIP code and equipment category without navigating multiple directories.

1

Enter Your ZIP Code

Start by entering the five-digit ZIP code of the area where you want to find a medical equipment supplier.

2

Select Equipment

Choose the equipment category you are looking for, such as wheelchairs, walkers, oxygen equipment or CPAP.

3

Explore Supplier Results

Review matching supplier locations, business names, addresses and available contact information displayed in the results.

4

Contact the Supplier

Contact the supplier directly to confirm equipment availability, pricing, insurance acceptance and delivery arrangements.

Understanding Your Search Results

Search results are generated from publicly available supplier information published by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

The results help identify supplier locations associated with the ZIP code and equipment category selected in your search.

Please remember: A supplier appearing in your search results does not guarantee that the supplier currently stocks the equipment, serves your particular address, accepts your insurance or has immediate availability. Always contact the supplier directly before making arrangements.

Important Things to Confirm with Suppliers

Before choosing a supplier, we recommend confirming:

  • Whether the required equipment is currently available.
  • Whether the supplier serves your location.
  • Whether delivery or installation is offered.
  • Whether your insurance plan is accepted.
  • Whether a prescription or prior authorization is required.
  • What costs, copayments or other charges may apply.

Is DMEvia Affiliated with Medicare or CMS?

No. DMEvia is an independent supplier directory. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by CMS, Medicare or any government agency.

We organize publicly available information to make supplier discovery easier. We do not determine Medicare coverage, insurance eligibility or medical necessity.
