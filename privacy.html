Privacy Policy

How DMEvia handles information when you visit or contact us.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

At DMEvia, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, how contact enquiries are handled, and the role of third-party services we use.

1. Information We Collect

You can browse the DMEvia website and search publicly available supplier information without creating an account.

If you contact us through our Contact Us page, you may provide:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • The category of your enquiry.
  • The message or information you choose to include.

We use this information to review your enquiry, respond to you, address website issues and consider supplier or business-related requests.

2. Contact Form and Third-Party Processing

Our contact form uses FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. When you submit the form, the information you enter is transmitted to FormSubmit and processed so that your enquiry can be delivered to info@dmevia.com.

FormSubmit may process submitted information for delivery and spam prevention. Its published documentation states that form submissions may be retained for up to 30 days. Its own privacy policy explains additional information about its processing practices.

After delivery, a copy of your enquiry may remain in our email inbox and related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the enquiry.

FormSubmit operates independently of DMEvia. Its handling of information is also governed by its own policies:

  • FormSubmit Privacy Policy
  • FormSubmit Help and FAQs

3. Please Do Not Submit Sensitive Information

Important: Do not submit medical records, patient names, dates of birth, insurance identification numbers, prescriptions, payment card details, financial information or other sensitive personal or health information through our contact form.

DMEvia is a supplier directory and information platform. Our contact form is intended for general enquiries, supplier information corrections, partnership enquiries and website-related communication.

4. Public CMS Supplier Information

DMEvia uses publicly available supplier information from the U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) Medical Equipment Suppliers dataset.

This information may include supplier business names, addresses, telephone numbers, equipment categories and other publicly listed supplier details.

This is business and supplier information obtained from a public data source. It is not information submitted by visitors through our website.

We display this information to help visitors discover DME supplier locations. The underlying public dataset may change or contain outdated information. A listing does not guarantee current inventory, delivery coverage, insurance acceptance, eligibility or availability.

For more information about the source, visit the official CMS dataset .

5. Cookies, Analytics and Technical Information

DMEvia does not currently intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers on its website.

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like other website hosting services, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and access logs to operate, protect and maintain the website.

If analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to explain their use.

6. How We Use Information

Information submitted through our contact form may be used to:

  • Respond to general questions and enquiries.
  • Help visitors with supplier-directory questions.
  • Review requests to correct supplier information.
  • Respond to supplier partnership or business enquiries.
  • Investigate website errors and technical issues.
  • Maintain records of relevant business correspondence.

We do not sell contact-form information to third parties or use it to send unsolicited marketing messages.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information submitted through our contact form.

Information may be processed by service providers involved in operating the website and delivering enquiries, including our website hosting provider and FormSubmit.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, website or users.

8. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to limit the information collected through our website and to use it for the purposes described in this policy. However, no website, email system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact enquiries may remain in our email inbox for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant business records or address follow-up matters.

Third-party providers may have their own data retention periods and security practices, which are described in their respective policies.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted through our website, or to request correction or deletion where appropriate and legally possible.

We may need to retain certain information where required by law or where necessary to resolve an ongoing enquiry or maintain legitimate business records.

You can also contact the relevant third-party service provider about information it processes under its own privacy policy.

10. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including government data sources and third-party services. We do not control those websites or their privacy practices. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites they choose to visit.

11. Children's Privacy

DMEvia is not designed to collect personal information from children. We ask visitors not to submit children's personal or medical information through our contact form.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or information-handling practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a privacy-related request, contact us at:

Email: info@dmevia.com

Website: https://dmevia.com/
